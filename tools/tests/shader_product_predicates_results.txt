Production
----------
For fully distinct multiplicand lanes, native-RTZ/manual-FTZ vertex multiply and
MAD borrow zero-exponent predicates already emitted for input flushing. Reuse
the existing dot product helper; extend its width guard to1..4 and rename it
Sm3Products to describe both callers. Replace two FAbs, NMin and zero equality
with one logical OR of existing predicates. For unmodified operands this removes
three SPIR-V operations and19 words per product; already-absolute operands may
save fewer operations. Keep the result's signed FTZ and +0 correction for
SM3 zero/denormal times NaN/Inf; preserve NoContraction and the independent MAD
third operand's flush. Partial-identity masks retain their extraction/insertion
path. Identical products, pixel, software RTZ and hardware FTZ remain unchanged.

New production code is a typed feature/operand-mask projection onto an existing
C89 classified-flush emitter and existing typed product helper. No new math
algorithm, buffer, payload copy, allocation, lock, thread, diagnostic, sleep or
CPU/GPU roundtrip. Borrow existing immutable IDs in the compiler's own arena.
The compiler still allocates elsewhere. This is not an end-to-end zero-copy,
allocation-free or measured GPU execution/register-pressure improvement.
