Immutable shader binding snapshots — 2026-11-10

Production change
- Remove SPIR-V translation's long-lived binding mutex ownership. Each cold
  producer fills one final packed C89 owner and publishes an immutable pointer
  through libretro-common atomics. Consumers borrow the records until joined
  shader destruction; no publication payload copy or producer wait is added.
- Equivalent competing owners are disposed without changing the winner.
  Conflicting completed owners fail publication. A producer starting without
  bindings must compare its records even if another publisher finishes first.
- Ready native binding checks allocate nothing. A first snapshot still allocates,
  including empty bindings. Duplicate native vertex/pixel DXIL generation remains
  serialized by the explicitly named native-emission mutex; it is separate from
  binding publication. No persistent key/schema, driver option or cache default
  changes, and no new release diagnostics, counters, clocks or sleeps.

Limits
No gameplay, sustained content-close, savestate restore, certified-cold driver
cache or end-to-end first-correct-frame acceptance is established. A first binding
owner still allocates, and native emission still has its deduplication mutex.
Draw-side shader translation/module/PSO creation, compilation waits, blocking
admission and inaccurate stand-ins remain unresolved. This change removes a
translation serialization dependency; it does not establish stutter-free play.
