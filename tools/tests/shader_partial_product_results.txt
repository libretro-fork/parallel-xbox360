Production and ownership
------------------------
Extend native-RTZ/manual-FTZ vertex MUL/MAD predicate reuse to partial aliases.
The existing product helper already supports a per-component correction mask.
A C89 helper projects the original four-component difference mask into compact
result-vector order. Its full-mask path returns immediately; other masks take
at most four iterations. Identical components keep their uncorrected products;
only differing components select SM3 +0 for zero/subnormal multiplicands. Keep
ordered NoContraction arithmetic, signed input/output FTZ and independent MAD
third-input flushing. Other feature/stage paths retain the existing lowering.

This removes the native partial path's operand/product swizzles or extracts,
separate absolute/minimum/equality zero checks, and insertion/shuffle back into
the result. Lend the complete operands to the existing masked product emitter.
No new dynamic container, persistent state, thread, lock, release diagnostic,
sleep or GPU/CPU roundtrip. The new C89 projection itself allocates and copies
no payload. The shared SPIR-V builder still owns/allocates IR instructions and
interned constants, including a small bool-vector mask. This is not a claim of
zero allocation across compilation, lower GPU register pressure or fewer GPU
memory transfers. Runtime edits outside the new C89 helper are typed metadata
glue; existing component paths remain for software/pixel/hardware-FTZ modes.
