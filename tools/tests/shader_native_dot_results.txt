Ordered native-RTZ vertex dot products
2026-11-08
Parent: 48f34107025ec87a0916bc1cefc2dd8fd5c43e9c

Change
------
Vertex DP4/DP3/DP2ADD on hardware-RTZ paths emit one vector product instead of
independent scalar products. This shares vector input/output FTZ handling on
devices without native FTZ. Products still receive the original per-lane SM3
zero/NaN correction; additions remain scalar and left-to-right with NoContraction.
No OpDot, FMA, reassociation, dropped draw or approximate arithmetic. Pixel and
full-integer fallback lowering remain unchanged. Existing immutable operand IDs
are borrowed; no new payload copy, allocation owner, arena, lock or queue. The
translator and driver still allocate; allocation-free gameplay is not claimed.
